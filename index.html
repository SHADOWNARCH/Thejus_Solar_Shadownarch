About Thejus Solar

Salute to the Sun

The rays of Sun "Suryarasmi" are the most abundant source of energy available free of cost in this world, which energizes our team. In Vedas, "Surya Namaskar" has been depicted as a salute to the sun. Here, the team of THEJUS SOLAR also salutes the never-ending energy provider.

Solar power is clean, sustainable, and reliable. To reduce pollution, carbon emission, global warming, and to prevent the consequences of climatic changes, we must harness this unlimited power.

Our Mission

We are on the move to harvest the unlimited energy of the sun to efficiently utilize it for our day-to-day activities. Our team of hardworking engineers and technicians is dedicated to implementing solar power projects in both residential and commercial sectors.

We pride ourselves on understanding customer requirements and providing tailor-made solutions with excellence in quality.
